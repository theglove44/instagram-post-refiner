How to decide — Ignore decoration. Ask which room you’d want to open at 9pm to write three captions. The winner is the one that makes the draft/final split feel obvious and calm.
Axis Current A Ink B Night C Gallery
Mood Social neon Editorial desk Craft console Calm product
Light Dark Light warm Dark cool Light soft
Accent IG pink/purple Berry Amber Periwinkle
Type Outfit Fraunces + DM Sans Instrument + Mono Instrument Sans
Risk Generic IG clone Too “blog theme” Feels sparse if nav grows Could feel SaaS-generic
Best if… You want zero change Writing is the product You keep dark + want focus Daytime use / multi-user later

My read

Current over-indexes on Instagram brand chrome for a tool whose real job is voice, not reach charts. For this workshop-first build I’d pick A (Ink Room) if you want the product to feel like a personal writing practice, or B (Night Desk) if you want to stay dark and just strip the neon. C is the safest “product polish” option if the app grows back toward dashboards.

Say A, B, C, a hybrid (e.g. “B colours + A typography”), or “keep current with X fixed” and I’ll implement tokens + Workshop page refinements only.
