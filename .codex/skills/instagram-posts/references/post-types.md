# Post-type playbooks

## Restaurant, café, pub

Lead with standout dish, venue, or verdict. Explain occasion and decisions. Move through selected dishes rather than full menu. Compare Chris and Tommo naturally. Include value or disappointment when supplied. End with honest return intention.

## Home food and buffet

Lead with meal or playful disagreement. Explain why it happened. Name brands only when relevant. Describe process and result through personal preference. Keep domestic humour and imperfections.

## Recipe or hack

Lead with finished food. Give origin story briefly. Present steps in usable order. Keep timings, temperatures, quantities, and safety information exact. Include genuine tip or failure point. Invite trial without generic engagement bait.

## Drink

Lead with drink, bar, or unusual preference. Describe flavour in familiar language. Mention setting and price where relevant. Avoid tasting-note theatre unless Chris supplied it.

## Travel or event

Lead with destination or defining moment. Tell journey, expectation, change of plan, and human reaction. Include contradictions. Do not force food-review structure onto museum, theatre, or travel experience.

## Gifted or invited

Lead with genuine experience. State relationship clearly. Keep criticism possible. Never imply purchase when gifted or independence when paid. Use supplied disclosure wording or clear equivalent.

## Reel

Keep first line compact and immediately legible. Let visual do work; cut scene description already obvious from video. Retain one useful story, tip, or verdict. Do not force every feed-caption convention.
