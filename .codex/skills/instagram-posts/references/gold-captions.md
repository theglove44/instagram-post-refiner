## Home recipe: buffalo wings

### Input shape

- Home carousel in Littleborough
- Best buffalo wings Chris and Tommo had made
- £2 Tesco wings
- Tefal air fryer
- Seasoning and timed cooking method
- BrewDog buffalo sauce and butter
- Crispy skin and meat pulling off bone
- Tommo loved them despite not normally liking wings

### Gold output

The best buffalo wings we've EVER had came out of our own air fryer. What's your secret wing recipe?

We fancied a wing night so decided to have a go at making our own, and it turns out a £2 pack of wings from Tesco and our trusty Tefal air fryer is all you need.

Pat them dry, spray with a bit of oil, then raid the spice cupboard.

We went salt and pepper (standard), garlic granules, onion granules, smoked paprika, a pinch of chinese five spice and a dusting of cornflour for that crispy skin.

Preheat the air fryer at 200c for a few minutes, wings in for 15, turn them, then another 15.

Then the good bit. Smother them in BrewDog Wingman Buffalo Sauce with a couple of teaspoons of butter mixed through, get everything coated, and back in for a final 2 minutes 🧑‍🍳

The meat was pulling clean off the bone and that buttery sauce clings to every single wing.

Even Tommo loved them, and she's not normally a wing fan, so that tells you everything.

Give them a go this weekend and let us know how you get on.

- Saves strongest texture and Tommo reaction for payoff.

## Hidden bar: Berry and Rye

Chris explicitly supplied this as his final version.

Ever walked past a bar twice thinking it was shut down? That's what happened outside BERRY AND RYE in Liverpool.

No signage, no lights, just blacked out windows and a door that looks like it hasn't opened in years. We knocked, waited, and got let in, it's tiny inside.

Stepping in though, it's such a buzz, dark and atmospheric with rows of whiskey and bourbon behind the bar, live jazz piano playing most nights too 🎹

Even the cocktail menu is an old book you can buy as a souvenir, and they do flights too, three small measures of a classic plus a couple of twists for £20 🤔 one for next time maybe 🤭

We shared a snack mix to start, it was fab, pretzels had us straight back to Norm off Cheers nibbling away at the end of the bar 😂, olives sat in a glass of ice to keep chilled, and corn nuts that were moorish.

I had the CORNBALLER, buttery and silky smooth, right up my street, while Tommo went for the Suanala with spiralled cucumber and a dash of chilli oil.

Being a massive Yellowstone fan I couldn't resist the YELLOWSTONE SELECT too 😋, while Tommo tried the Pisco Punch but reckons her first pick still won.

Cocktails aren't cheap, but it's one guy making them with decent measures of quality booze, which we reckon is worth paying for.

If you're ever wandering round Liverpool, it's well worth finding this place and knocking to see if they're home.

## Mixed restaurant visit: The Rake

A bit much when you just want to graze in peace.

Left full and happy though, which is what counts.
