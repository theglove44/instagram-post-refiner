## Remove empty engagement hook

Before: `Ever crossed town for a bagel? The RAINBOW BAGEL...`

After: `The RAINBOW BAGEL at Wildgoose was worth every step of Hebden Bridge in 31 degree heat 🌈`

Lesson: specific declarative beats question added only for engagement.

## Add lived humour

Before: `...the day after England v Mexico, so we were taking it nice and steady.`

After: `...the day after England v Mexico. No we didn't stay up to watch it. The highlights is the future 😂`

Lesson: use supplied behaviour and self-aware aside; avoid generic scene-setting.

## Expand honest verdict

Before: `We really liked the place and the service was spot on.`

After: `We really liked the place and the service was spot on. The deli is a brilliant touch and the food was nice just a couple of little niggles that left us thinking it was so close to be brilliant 🤦‍♂️`

Lesson: retain mixed judgement and explain emotional landing.

## Use emotional pacing

Before: `The most haunting is an actual life jacket worn by one of the survivors. That one stopped us both in our tracks.`

After:

`The most haunting is an actual life jacket worn by one of the survivors.`

`That one stopped us both in our tracks 🥺`

Lesson: whitespace and restrained emoji can carry emotion better than extra adjectives.

## Prefer specific context

Before: `Bank holiday Monday and we jumped on the train into Tod for our first visit...`

After: `Bank holiday Monday and we jumped on the train into Tod for a bit of a drink and food afternoon...`

Lesson: state actual intention and activity, not generic visit language.

## Keep opinion personal

Before: `that sauce was something else.`

After: `that sauce was a delight 🤩`

Lesson: concise personal reaction can be stronger than stock food-copy wording.
